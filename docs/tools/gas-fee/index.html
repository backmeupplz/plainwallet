Ethereum gas fee calculator

A transaction fee is the gas it uses times the price of each unit of gas, and the price is quoted in gwei. A plain ETH transfer uses 21,000 gas, so at 30 gwei it costs 630,000 gwei, which is 0.00063 ETH. Pick Ethereum or Base, read the current fee from a public node or type your own, and see the cost in gwei, ETH and dollars.

The fields hold example numbers until you read the current fee or change them.

How the fee is worked out

Since the London upgrade (EIP-1559) every block has a base fee, set by the protocol from how full the previous blocks were, and it is burned. On top of it you may offer a priority fee, which goes to the block producer. What you pay per unit of gas is the base fee plus the priority fee, and the fee is that price times the gas the transaction actually uses. The number a wallet shows before you send is usually a maximum: a transaction pays the base fee of its own block, which can be lower than the cap you set, and any difference is not charged.

This page multiplies exact integers: your gwei values become whole numbers of wei, and the fee is shown in gwei, ETH and, using the ETH price in the box, dollars. The dollar figure is approximate because the price moves; edit it to any price you want to test.

Where the current fee comes from

The button asks a public node, PublicNode, for the latest block's base fee and for its suggested priority fee, and asks the network's Chainlink ETH/USD price feed (read with a plain contract call on the same node) for the price. That is a read-only request from your browser: no wallet, no address and nothing you typed is sent, but the node does see your IP address as any website does. If the node does not answer within five seconds, the fields keep your numbers and you can type your own.

Base is a layer 2, and its fee has a second part this page leaves out: the cost of posting the transaction's data to Ethereum, which is added to the fee on top of the amount shown here and is usually small next to Ethereum's fee. Fees on both networks can change from one block to the next, and the figure is an estimate, not a quote.

Worked examples

I ran these through this page's own code on November 15, 2026 with a made-up price of 30 gwei (base fee 29 plus a tip of 1) and ETH at $3,000. The figures are examples, not today's fees.

  • ETH transfer, 21,000 gas: 630,000 gwei, which is 0.00063 ETH, or $1.89.
  • Token transfer, 65,000 gas: 1,950,000 gwei, which is 0.00195 ETH, or $5.85.
  • Swap, 150,000 gas: 4,500,000 gwei, which is 0.0045 ETH, or $13.50.
  • The same ETH transfer at 0.01 gwei, a price Base and other layer 2 networks often have: 210 gwei, which is 0.00000021 ETH, or less than $0.001.
